Step by step (same rules as the desktop Sale Return, menu 13)

1 · Select shop

Return opens from Customer detail and from the Visit screen. Company and branch come from Switch company, as for Sales and Receipt. The shop's bills, open bills, the return reasons and the branch's godowns come from the last Sync, and the screens say “as of” that time. The shop can change only until a bill is picked (the desktop locks the customer then).

2 · Pick the bill (2b · No bill)

Posted bills of the shop for the last [90] days, newest first, each with how many lines can still come back; search by bill no., item or amount; fully returned bills hidden unless ticked. A bill past sales.return_window_days is marked (VK Nex holds that return for the office). No bill only when sales.free_return_allowed is on: the rate is VK Nex's (last sale inside the window, else today's price), and an item found on a synced bill offers that bill instead.

3 · Return items (3a · line)

The reason first, once (VK Nex's six return reasons); its default condition fills new lines. Qty in any unit of the item, checked in base units against can-return (billed − posted returns − this return's other parts). Condition per line: Good, Damaged, Expired, Scrap; one bill line can be split (3 good + 2 damaged). All back fills every line. Free scheme lines come back at ₹0.00. The bill's lot comes along; batch and expiry are typed only on a no-bill line.

4 · Review (4a · adjust)

An estimate from the bill line (goods and GST pro rata, the reason's charge rule, round off), labelled as such. Settle by Adjust (this bill, then oldest; by hand on 4a) or Keep as credit note, starting at VK Nex's default (sales.return_settle_default). Cash refund stays in the office. Goods go into the bill line's godown or one picked from the branch. Shop's debit note no. and date, remarks. Return date is today.

5 · Save on phone

A confirm that lists the goods to load, good apart from damaged. Saved with a client id and a phone ref.; with Adjust the shop's balance on the phone drops at once. A slip to share, marked “not a credit note yet”. It can be edited or deleted until it is sent. The goods stay in the van until VK Nex posts the return.

6 · Sync

POST /mobile/returns/create after bills and before receipts. VK Nex saves the draft (srId = the client id, so a lost answer is resent safely), works out the money from the bill line and posts. If it can't post, the draft stays in VK Nex with the reason: With the office (approval, a warning to override, no Post right on menu 13) or Sent back (a hard refusal such as SALES_RETURN_OVER_QTY), which the salesman fixes and sends again into the same draft.

7 · Posted in VK Nex

The SRT number, the final credit note and how it settled come back: bills adjusted, or the credit the shop now holds (which Receipt's dues then shows as a credit to use). Stock comes in at the bill line's cost: Good to saleable, Damaged and Expired to their own stock, Scrap to quarantine. Open bills and the shop's balance are refreshed in the same Sync.

8–9 · Return list and detail

A Return list tile on Customer detail (that shop; Today, This week, This month), like Receipt list. Status comes from GET /mobile/returns/status at each Sync: posted, approved, cancelled or amended in the office. It totals the goods still in the van, good and damaged apart. Posted returns change only in the office.
