Step 2 · Decisions for the new sales return

Not answered yet. The screens on this page are drawn with the recommended answer; say which to change. They replace the defaults of the page 6 phone build (17 Nov): free return, reason per line, phone-only numbers, never sent.

D1

Against a bill or not

Recommended: A bill by default, as on the desktop. No bill only when sales.free_return_allowed is on: VK Nex prices it (last sale inside the window, else today's price) and the office approves it (RETURN_NO_RECEIPT).

D2

Who works out the money

Recommended: VK Nex, as on the desktop (rule D45). The phone sends qty, unit, condition and notes, shows an estimate from the bill line, and VK Nex's figure replaces it after Sync.

D3

Reason

Recommended: One per return and required, from VK Nex's six return reasons. Its default condition fills new lines; a line can still differ (Good, Damaged, Expired, Scrap). The two claim reasons stay in the office (D8).

D4

How the credit note is settled

Recommended: Adjust (this bill, then oldest) or Keep as credit note, starting at VK Nex's default. No cash refund from the phone: VK Nex needs a till session, cash in the drawer and the REFUND_CASH approval.

D5

Godown the goods go into

Recommended: The bill line's godown by default (the desktop's default). The salesman may pick another godown of the branch for the whole return, as for phone bills; VK Nex checks it belongs to the branch.

D6

Adjust split

Recommended: VK Nex's own plan unless the salesman changes it on 4a. A bill paid in the office before Sync: VK Nex moves that row to the next oldest bill or keeps it as credit, instead of refusing SALES_RETURN_ADJUST_STALE (as receipts D1).

D7

Post at once, or a draft for the office

Recommended: Post at once at Sync, like phone bills and receipts. When VK Nex can't (an approval; a warning such as SALES_RETURN_WINDOW, _LOT_EXPIRED, _SCHEME_SHORT; no Post right on menu 13), it stays a DRAFT for the office. Other choice: always a draft, posted when the goods reach the godown.

D8

Claims (goods destroyed at the shop)

Recommended: Office only. The phone sends Kind RETURN and the goods always come back in the van.

D9

Which bills the phone keeps

Recommended: The shop's posted bills of the last [90] days, or sales.return_window_days when it is set, downloaded at Sync with what can still come back. An older bill is returned with no bill.

D10

Date, charges, salesman

Recommended: Date always today. Freight and loading come back by the reason's rule, with no ticks on the phone. Salesmen, loadmen and vehicle are copied from the bill; with no bill, the login's own employee.

D11

After Sync

Recommended: Posted returns change only in the office (amend or cancel). A Sent back return can be fixed and sent again into the same VK Nex draft, or deleted, which deletes that draft too.

D12

Numbers and sharing

Recommended: Phone ref. R… until Sync, then VK Nex's SRT number. The shop's debit note no. goes in srUsrRefno (the desktop meaning), so the phone ref. needs its own column. Share the slip as text now; print later.
