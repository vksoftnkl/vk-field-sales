What changed from the old Salesman app, and why

The All Screens Review shows 57 screens of the old Qt/QML app on 10 pages. The new phone app has 20, plus one set of messages. Each one uses VK Nex's own names, fields and rules, so an order, bill, receipt or return made on the phone looks the same in VK Nex desktop and web as one keyed in the office.

Advance receipt → Receipt
VK Nex keeps money beyond the open bills on account. No more dummy bill "123".
Payment types + Add payment type → Receipt
Payment rows sit inside the receipt, with each VK Nex tender's reference rule.
Date range + Custom dates + Calendar → chips
Range chips and the phone's own date picker. One tap instead of three screens.
8 reports + receipt approval → Reports list
One Reports list with one date strip; each report opens Day close, My documents, Dues, Ledger or Stock. Full reports stay in VK Nex desktop and web.
Settings → a smaller Settings screen
Only phone things stay; VK Nex rules show read-only. Beat mode and item mode are gone: beat = area, and items filter by group and brand.

Removed

Choose company / receipt by company or group
The company comes from the VK Nex login. A VK Nex receipt belongs to one company, and VK Nex has no receipt-type setting.
Second salesman picker
VK Nex keeps one salesman per document in practice (the first id in so_salesman_id).
Bill save confirmation popup
The review screen is the confirmation, for both the order and the bill.
Rate history (needed internet)
No VK Nex route exists for it. Past bills are in the synced ledger.
Stock refresh button
Stock arrives with Sync, and the phone stays offline.
Freight and cooly on phone documents
The mobile order and bill routes refuse charges. The office adds them.
Printing on the phone
VK Nex numbers the bill only at Sync. This is open: see decision 3.
Phone bill numbers that restarted daily
VK Nex gives the number (bil…). The phone's own number is kept as the user ref no (sbUsrRefno).
Route map, wireless printer setup
RouteMap.qml was empty, and printing is out (above).

VK Nex rules the phone follows

PriceStart from the customer's price level (WS, Retail …). Take off the customer rate (csrDiscQty), then use the qty slab if it is lower. A typed rate needs the Edit price right. A rate below Min is refused; one above MRP is refused unless skip-MRP is on.
SchemesOnly APPROVED, auto-apply schemes with no coupon. They are matched by customer, group, area or city, by item, brand or group, and by branch, date, weekday and bill type. Sales bills only: the VK Nex Sales Order runs no scheme engine.
GSTCGST + SGST when the POS state equals the company state, otherwise IGST. GST is rounded to paise per line, then split. The total is rounded by sales.round_off_step, and VK Nex refuses a bill whose total is off by more than ₹0.01.
CreditThree tests: amount over the limit, open bills at the bill limit, and oldest bill older than the credit days. On an order, the salesman is asked "Order on credit anyway?". On a bill, REFUSE blocks and WARN needs the override right. With Credit allowed = No, there is no Credit term.
Numbers and statusVK Nex numbers everything at Sync (sor…, bil…). Orders arrive as Draft, and the office confirms them (Post right on Sales Order). Bills and receipts are posted at once. Returns arrive as Draft.
SalesmanThe salesman is the login's employee (employee_master). That id goes on orders, bills, receipts and visits: so_salesman_id, sb_salesman_id, avh_employee_id and cv_salesman_id.
