Sync and VK Nex APIs

The phone talks to VK Nex only when the salesman taps Sync. Every route is under /api/v1. Routes marked NEW do not exist yet; they are what the new workflow needs from the ERP server's mobile module.

Bills go before receipts, so today's bill is open when VK Nex allocates the receipt. Every upload carries a client uuid and deviceUid, so a resend after a lost answer is safe. The phone keeps each refusal next to its record and shows it on the Sync page.

Fix in the routes that exist

receipts/options requires deviceUid. A phone that isn't registered leaves it out, gets a 400, and its receipt data stops downloading.
receipts/create refuses avhEmployeeId. If accounts.receipt_salesman_mandatory is turned on, every phone receipt is refused. Fill the salesman from item 1 above.
visits/create requires cvReasonCode. The phone sends one only when it has one. The new No order screen makes the reason required.
The phone's bill maths differs from VK Nex. Today it takes the discount before tax and always uses CGST+SGST. VK Nex refuses a total that is off by more than ₹0.01 (AMOUNT_MISMATCH). Use the web app's line engine.
The ledger is fetched per customer, per year. That is two calls for every shop on every Sync. A since-date or batch ledger route would cut this.
