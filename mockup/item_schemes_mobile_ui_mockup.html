How it works

  • New badges on the rows of items an offer names (the item, its group, brand, category or section). Green = applied now, outlined = not reached yet.
  • Offers on the whole order (no item list) show once, in the banner, with how much more is needed.
  • Only offers that suit this shop, this branch and today show. Sync brings them; the refresh button brings them with the prices.
  • Tap a badge for the offer’s details (screen 4).

How it works

  • Each line shows its offer and the money it took off. Free goods are their own lines (green, FREE, ₹0.00), under the line that earned them.
  • Free lines can’t be edited or deleted; they follow the quantities. Apply offers off takes every offer off this order (the desktop’s Promotions box).
  • Totals gain a Scheme discount row (and an Order offer row when one applies). Delivery date, priority and remarks stay as they are.
  • Sync sends the offer lines to VK Nex with the order.

How it works

  • New sheet: everything VK Nex says about the offer, in plain words: what it gives, dates and days, unit, shops, branches, how it mixes with other offers.
  • Slab offers list every band, with the current one marked.
  • Read from the phone’s own copy, so it works with no signal.
  • An offer that ended since the last Sync no longer shows or applies (the phone checks the order date).

To decide before building (full wording and recommendations in the plan)

  1. Billing a phone order: keep the offer the phone gave, or check it again on the bill date (today’s desktop way)? Recommended: check again.
  2. Offer stopped after the last Sync: should VK Nex refuse such an order? Recommended: no, accept it; the bill checks.
  3. Shop-group and city offers: the desktop bill can’t match them today. Fix the desktop too, so both agree? Recommended: yes.
  4. Loyalty points: earned only on a posted bill. Show the shop’s points and “earns points” on the phone later, but no points on orders? Recommended: yes.
  5. Salesman control: offers on/off per line and per order only; no typing over offer figures or free qty? Recommended: yes.
  6. Phone bills and returns: same offers on phone bills (later phase); none on returns? Recommended: yes.
  7. Cash-only offers: phone orders are always credit, so cash-only offers never show. OK? Recommended: yes.
  8. Free goods short in stock: keep the free line as a back-order (warn, don’t block), like paid lines? Recommended: yes.
